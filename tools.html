Tools

Pick an area to see its equipment and how to get access. Most areas need an orientation before you start.

  • Labeled GREEN

    General Tools

    Hand tools, glues, cutting mats and craft supplies for everyday making.

  • Pink entrance

    Wood Shop

    Table saw, band saw, drill press and hand tools. Needs its own orientation.

  • Labeled YELLOW

    Sewing & Textiles

    Sewing machines, a 4-thread serger, an industrial sewing machine and fabric tools.

  • Labeled TEAL

    Electronics

    Solder irons, bench power supplies, an oscilloscope and Arduino / Raspberry Pi parts.

  • Labeled RED

    Lasers

    Two 75 watt CO2 laser cutters for wood, plastic, paper and fabric. Needs its own orientation.

  • Labeled PURPLE

    3D Printers

    Prusa Mk4, Mk3S+ and MINI2 FDM printers running PLA and ABS.

  • Labeled BLUE

    CNC

    A Shaper Origin CNC router. A brief training is required.
