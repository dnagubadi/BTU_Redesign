Wood Shop

The Wood Shop entrance is marked pink. Inside, tools follow their own color-coded system.

Access & Orientation

Wood Shop access is separate from general BTU access and requires a dedicated orientation, regardless of past experience. The orientation schedule is posted on the BTU Lab Calendar and is run by the BTU Mischief Makers.

Access to certain machines is facilitated by BTU Mischief Makers — experienced community members and staff who can help you learn. Anyone can become a Mischief Maker on any machine.

For safety, major equipment (table saw, band saw, belt sander, chop saw, and drill press) is powered off daily between 10pm and 6am. Tampering with the power timers is considered a serious safety violation.

Safety Policies

  • Observe all safety rules without exception.
  • Do not work alone when no one could hear you if you needed assistance — stay in earshot.
  • Do not operate any tool you are unfamiliar with; get assistance instead.
  • Do not alter any machine in a permanent way.
  • Do not remove tools from the wood shop or BTU Lab without staff approval.
  • Do not attempt to repair a malfunctioning tool — report it instead.
  • BTU staff reserve the right to revoke wood shop access for any reason deemed necessary.
  • Deadlines and fatigue do not justify ignoring wood shop policy.
  • Repeated policy violations can result in reduced access for everyone, including locked machines or lab closures when staff are unavailable.
